What goes wrong if the value is a string, for example `target = "Asia"` and `df.query(f"continent == {target}")`? ## `GROUP BY` and `HAVING` (10.4)

Explain why `WHERE` is allowed to test the average here when it was not allowed to in notebook 10.4. What is the practical cost of this approach compared to using `HAVING`?
